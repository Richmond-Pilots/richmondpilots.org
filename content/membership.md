{{< section >}}
To become a prospective member, you must fill out the [Membership Application](/docs/RPC_Membership_Application.pdf), email it to {{< contact-email >}}, and bring a $100 deposit to a monthly meeting.

After becoming a prospective member, you will join the waitlist until a slot opens. Sometimes the waitlist is very short (about a month), and sometimes it can be several months.

We highly encourage all prospective members to attend our monthly meetings.

{{< meeting >}}
{{< /section >}}

{{< section title="Can I learn to fly with the club?" alt="true" >}}
The club has CFI/CFIIs available weekdays and weekends. Additionally, any non-member CFI can be checked out to teach in club aircraft provided the instruction is for members only. The club routinely admits students with no experience at all.
