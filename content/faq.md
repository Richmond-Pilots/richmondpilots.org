{{< faq q="Can a student pilot join?" >}}
We accept all pilots and student pilots. No minimum hours required. The club routinely admits students with no experience at all.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq q="Do I need my own flight instructor?" >}}
The club has CFI/CFIIs available weekdays and weekends. Additionally, any non-member CFI can be checked out to teach in club aircraft provided the instruction is for members only.
